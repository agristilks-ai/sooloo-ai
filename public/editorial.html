Editorial policy

TILKSWEB develops original digital media and researches technology, science, business and other topics. We distinguish independently verified facts, research findings, estimates and fictional scenarios.

Claims made by companies or founders are marked as their claims until we can check them. We do not invent numbers, quotes or results.

AI tools may be used throughout research, writing, illustration, narration and production. Our editorial standard requires human approval before publication. Synthetic content is disclosed where appropriate.

Affiliate links and sponsored content will always be labelled. A sponsor never decides what we write about a product.

Found a mistake? We correct it and note the correction on the page.
