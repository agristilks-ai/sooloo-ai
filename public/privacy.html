Privacy

This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

Free tools. The recipes, prices and numbers you type into tilksweb tools stay in your own browser. They are not sent to us.

Email for a tool extra. If you give your email to unlock something in a tool (for example printable recipe cost cards), we store your email address, which tool it came from and the time you agreed. We use it only to send you new free kitchen tools and tips from tilksweb. We do not sell or share it. It is stored in a Cloudflare database (Cloudflare, Inc. hosts this site). We keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

Contact. The name and email address of the person responsible for your data will be shown here.
